## Summary

Cosmos Semantic, as distributed through this Azure Marketplace offer, is an **Azure Managed Application**. When you deploy it, every resource it creates (the container running the server, its Key Vault, its managed identity) is created **inside your own Azure subscription**, and runs there under your own control. [PUBLISHER LEGAL NAME] does not host, operate or have access to your deployed instance, its data, its semantic model, or the queries your users or AI agents run against it.

This is different from most SaaS products: there is no multi-tenant backend operated by the publisher that your data passes through.

## What data [PUBLISHER LEGAL NAME] does and does not receive

**Does not receive:**

- The content of your semantic model (metrics, business rules, glossary).
- The data in the databases you connect (Databricks, Snowflake, BigQuery, Postgres, etc.).
- Query results, audit logs, or usage/cost records generated by your deployed instance. These stay inside your subscription's Container App, Key Vault, and (if you enable it) your own logging destination.
- Credentials for your data sources or for Microsoft Entra ID. These are configured directly in your own deployment (via environment variables and your Key Vault), never sent to the publisher.

**May receive**, through the normal Azure Marketplace transaction flow, not through the product itself:

- Your subscription ID and tenant ID, as part of the standard Marketplace purchase/usage event that Microsoft reports to every commercial Marketplace publisher for billing and support purposes.
- Information you provide directly if you contact support (e.g. an email you send, a GitHub issue you file).

## Microsoft Entra ID authentication

The deployed instance authenticates API and MCP calls against **your own** Microsoft Entra ID tenant, using the issuer and audience you configure at deployment. [PUBLISHER LEGAL NAME] is not a party to that authentication; it happens entirely between your deployed instance and your own tenant.

## Data retention and deletion

Since no customer data is held by the publisher, there is nothing for the publisher to retain or delete on your behalf. Deleting the Managed Application resource (and its managed resource group) in your Azure subscription removes everything the deployment created.
