## Post

Your AI agent just gave you a number. Can you prove it's right?

"Revenue" has three definitions. The agent picks one, and nobody knows which.

Cosmos Semantic is an open-source semantic layer with an ontology at its core:
→ define metrics, rules and concepts once
→ serve them to agents over MCP and to apps over REST
→ attach the evidence to every answer: formula, rules, filters, reference date and hashes

The same number on Databricks, Snowflake, Oracle, SQL Server, PostgreSQL, MySQL, MariaDB and DuckDB.

Swipe through the 9 slides, then try the live demo (no install):
https://wiliamrosa.github.io/cosmos-semantic-platform/
