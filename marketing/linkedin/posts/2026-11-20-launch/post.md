# Friday 2026-11-20: launch

The page's first post: what Cosmos Semantic is, for people who have never heard of it.

## Post

Meet Cosmos Semantic: an open-source semantic layer that makes AI agents get the number right, and prove it.

The problem: an AI agent that writes SQL straight over your tables, without your business rules, returns numbers that look right and that nobody can explain. "Revenue" has three definitions. "Active customer" means 60 days to one team and 90 to another.

Cosmos Semantic defines metrics, business rules and concepts once, and serves them to agents over MCP instead of letting them guess SQL. Every answer carries its evidence: the expanded formula, the rules applied, the filters and the reference date.

One model, the same number on Databricks, Snowflake, Oracle, SQL Server, PostgreSQL, MySQL, MariaDB and DuckDB, checked by the test suite on every change.
