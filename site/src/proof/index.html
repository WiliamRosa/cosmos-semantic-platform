Stop paying for data nobody reads, and prove it.

CosmosProof shows what in your Azure Databricks costs money, creates risk or holds PII without being used. It crosses Unity Catalog lineage with ADLS Gen2 storage activity, and only calls something unused when the evidence agrees.

How it works

Discover. Cross the evidence. Prove each finding.

  1. 1

    Discover

    Every Azure Databricks workspace and ADLS Gen2 account in the subscriptions or management group you choose, through Azure Resource Graph. Development and POC start unselected.

  2. 2

    Cross the evidence

    Unity Catalog says who reads each table; ADLS Gen2 says when each file was last read, by any tool. A finding needs both to agree. If storage shows a read, there is no finding.

  3. 3

    Prove

    Each finding lists the evidence, what could not be seen, a confidence level and a proof hash. Run after run, new findings are alerts and resolved ones are savings.

One map, three readings

Cost, risk and PII, from the same evidence.

Cost

Tables and files nobody reads, sized from storage, with the estimated monthly cost and the cheaper tier they could move to. With Unity Catalog system tables, also the jobs and pipelines still feeding them, with their compute cost.

Risk

Production credentials and federated connections that exist with no real use: access that nobody needs and somebody could misuse.

PII

Personal data such as tax IDs, e-mails and names, stored in tables nobody reads. Recognized from column names; the values are never read.

How confidence works

high
No read in lineage and storage access covers the window and shows no read.
medium
No read in lineage and storage cannot confirm; or storage no table owns, with storage confirming no read.
low
Storage no table owns, with no storage confirmation.

The app

For the people who decide, not only for engineers.

A Databricks App in English and Portuguese. People sign in with the workspace's existing single sign-on: no app registration, no new user database. Light and dark mode.

Security

Built to pass a bank's security review.

Read-only

CosmosProof never deletes, moves or changes your data. Its only write is to its own reports container. cosmosproof grants prints every read-only grant for a person to review and run.

Inside your tenant

A managed identity in your subscription, with no stored credential. It reads metadata, never the contents of tables or files. Nothing is sent to us.
